How this creates value

  • Creates a prioritized action view.
  • Connects gaps to business impact.
  • Separates symptoms from root causes.
  • Supports governance and progress tracking.

What this prevents

  • Action plans with no priorities.
  • Fixing symptoms only.
  • Ignoring ownership gaps.
  • Losing findings after workshops.
