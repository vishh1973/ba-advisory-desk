04 Risks, Controls, And Actions

Board pack quality controls.

The deck is checked for executive clarity, decision logic, and evidence discipline.

ControlQuestionRisk reducedAction
Message testCan the slide headline stand alone?Weak narrativeUse action headline.
Decision testDoes the deck ask for a clear decision?No outcomeAdd decision page.
Evidence testIs every key claim supported?Credibility riskAdd source note.
Length testCan it be reviewed within board time?OverloadMove detail to appendix.
Risk testAre risks grouped by decision relevance?Unfocused concernUse risk themes.
Follow up testDoes the roadmap name owners?No execution pathAdd owner view.
