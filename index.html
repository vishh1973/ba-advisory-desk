Credit guide

1 Advisory Credit equals one defined Business Analysis delivery unit agreed before work starts. Examples include a requirements clarity pack, SOP package, UAT scenario pack, decision brief, stakeholder synthesis, or vendor readiness package.

Larger outcomes may use multiple credits. Additional 3 credit blocks are available for $1,000 and expire 30 days after purchase.
