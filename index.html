About Us

A Business Analysis consortium built for high quality client delivery and clear outcomes.

BA Advisory Desk is built around a consortium model of highly experienced business analysts, transformation architects, delivery leaders, and advisory specialists who understand how complex work gets approved, funded, scoped, governed, and delivered.

We support software teams, consulting firms, transformation offices, product leaders, and operational teams that need polished Business Analysis deliverables without adding permanent headcount or entering a large consulting engagement.

The service is designed for geographically distributed support so client deliverables can be handled across time zones with practical response discipline and global coverage.

Credit guide

1 Advisory Credit equals one defined Business Analysis delivery unit agreed before work starts. Examples include a requirements clarity pack, SOP package, UAT scenario pack, decision brief, stakeholder synthesis, or vendor readiness package.

Larger outcomes may use multiple credits. The 3 Advisory Credit Top Up is available for $1,000 USD and expires 30 days after purchase.
