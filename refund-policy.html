Refund Policy

Clear refund rules for advisory work.

Last updated: May 19, 2026

BA Advisory Desk provides professional advisory time, document review, synthesis, and deliverable preparation. Refunds are reviewed based on the package purchased, the work stage, and the terms accepted at checkout or invoice.

Before Work Begins

If no triage, review, planning, analysis, or advisory work has started, you may ask us to review cancellation and refund options.

After Work Begins

Once work has started, fees are generally not refundable because advisory capacity has been reserved and professional review has begun. If there is a delivery concern, we may offer a correction, replacement, partial credit, or another remedy at our discretion.

Monthly Plans And Credits

Monthly plan fees cover reserved advisory capacity, workspace management, request handling, and included monthly credits. Unused monthly credits expire according to the plan terms. Credit top ups expire 30 days after purchase unless a written custom arrangement says otherwise.

If a refund is approved for a payment that added Advisory Credits, unused credits tied to that payment may be removed or adjusted. Credits already used or reserved for active work may not be available for refund or removal until the work is reviewed.

Custom Advisory Work

Custom advisory engagements follow the accepted proposal or invoice. If that document includes a separate cancellation or refund rule, that rule applies.

Payment Issues

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, contact support@baadvisorydesk.com. Include the account email, company name, invoice date, payment amount, and a short explanation. Refunds, disputes, and charge reviews are handled by support before any workspace credit balance is changed.
